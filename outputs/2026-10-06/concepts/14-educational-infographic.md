**Template**: Educational Infographic — **Tier**: B — **Role**: mid-funnel supporting
cast, under-used; reads as content
**Angle**: Where a small nonprofit's donor, grant, and finance data actually lives —
and how one platform closes the loop
**CTA / landing**: Try Kindora free at cad.spaceaiapp.com/kindora

**Headline (core)**: A typical small nonprofit runs data in four separate places.
Here is how they come together in one.

**Grounded in**: R5 Amanda Foster (all-in-one, "we don't need to switch between
different tools") + R3 Priya Sharma (grant cost allocation across grants) + homepage
claim "removes the need for 3-4 separate subscriptions and the manual data entry
between them."

> Educational framing raises the truth bar: every claim in the graphic is the platform's
> documented workflow (donor records → donations → comms; grant proposals → receipts →
> allocation → compliance reports; clients → services → outcomes; finances → statement of
> activities). No invented numbers or mechanisms.

(Donors/CRM, Grants, Client Programs, Finances) each with its own data entry arrow and a
"manual re-entry" dashed red arrow between them. On the right, one box labeled Kindora
with a circular flow: Donation → Follow-up → Case → Cost allocation → Funder report.
Caption: "Four tools, one loop." Logo small at bottom right.
