## Critical Risk: Entity Disambiguation

The name "SpaceNexus" is **ambiguous**, which actively suppresses AI recognition:

1. **spacenexus.us** — an unrelated "SpaceNexus LLC" (Houston, TX) running a *space industry intelligence* platform. Same name, different company, different domain. AI systems may conflate or misattribute.
2. **"Nexus"** as a token is dominated by Nexus Mods, Google Nexus, Starcom: Nexus, etc.

Without a disambiguating entity anchor (Wikidata, Wikipedia, or consistent third-party mentions tied to the exact domain), AI models cannot reliably resolve "SpaceNexus" to the VDR product.

## Recommendations

### Immediate Actions (Week 1–2)

1. **Wikipedia/Wikidata:** Create a **Wikidata item** (Q-number) immediately — this does not require full notability debate and gives AI a machine-readable entity anchor. Include official website, founded date, industry, and founder. This is the single cheapest high-leverage fix.
2. **YouTube:** Publish 3–5 short videos targeting comparison/alternative queries (e.g., "Datasite vs SpaceNexus," "Best VDR for startups"). Optimize titles, descriptions, and spoken mentions of "SpaceNexus" so transcripts index the exact brand.
3. **Review platforms:** Launch a genuine review-collection campaign on G2 and Capterra. Zero reviews is a trust gap AI systems penalize for software recommendation queries.

### Short-Term Strategy (Month 1–3)

1. **Reddit:** Identify 3–5 relevant subreddits (r/mergersandacquisitions, r/privateequity, r/startups, r/lawfirm). Participate authentically; get the product mentioned in "what VDR do you recommend" threads. Zero Reddit presence is the largest single gap.
2. **Earned PR:** Pursue coverage in M&A/legal-tech publications and podcasts. Recency matters — target mentions within the last 6 months. A single solid industry article far outweighs more self-published pages.
3. **Resolve the naming collision:** Add an explicit "SpaceNexus (VDR software, spacenexus.net)" disambiguation across your About page, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, and Wikidata to separate you from spacenexus.us.
4. **Founder visibility:** Build a citable founder entity (LinkedIn thought leadership, podcast appearances, bylined articles, and a complete Wikidata entry).

### Long-Term Authority Building (Month 3–12)

1. **Wikipedia article:** Build notability through press, industry recognition, and independent coverage first (never self-edit). A B-class article with references is the strongest possible AI entity signal.
2. **YouTube authority:** Guest appearances on M&A/fintech channels; build a library of comparison videos that AI cites for "alternatives" queries.
3. **Sustained Reddit + review presence:** Consistent, authentic community participation to compound the two highest-correlation signals.
