The page is well-structured and scannable, and its FAQ section is genuinely citable. It is held back almost entirely by two weaknesses: near-zero statistical density (the "stats band" is made of labels, not numbers) and mostly derivative, benefit-led marketing copy that lacks answer-first definitions. The two above-70 blocks are both FAQ answers; every marketing block falls below 70.

## Strongest Content Blocks

### 1. "What is grant management software?" -- Score: 74/100
> Grant management software helps nonprofits track the entire grant lifecycle — from discovery and application through award management, reporting, and closeout. It centralizes deadlines, documents, budgets, and compliance requirements in one system.

**Why it works:** Opens with a textbook definition pattern ("Grant management software helps nonprofits track..."), names its subject explicitly, and packs the full lifecycle into a single self-contained passage that maps directly to a high-volume AI query. Only the absence of any statistic keeps it out of the 80s.

### 2. "How does Kindora help with grant reporting?" -- Score: 72/100
> Kindora automates grant report generation by pulling financial data, program outcomes, and impact metrics from across the platform. You can generate narrative and financial reports with one click, saving hours of manual compilation.

**Why it works:** Answer-first, names the product and the actor, and contains a concrete mechanism ("pulling financial data, program outcomes, and impact metrics"). The "one click" claim is a specific product detail. It would score higher if "saving hours" were replaced with a verifiable time figure.

### 3. "Can Kindora track multiple grants at once?" -- Score: 69/100
> Yes. Kindora provides a pipeline view of all your grants — active applications, pending decisions, awarded grants, and closed grants. Each grant has its own detailed record with documents, budgets, tasks, and reporting deadlines.

**Why it works:** Question-based heading plus a direct "Yes." answer, followed by an enumerated feature list that stands alone without prior context. Missing only a quantified data point to clear 70.

### 4. Hero / Intro ("Streamline Your Grant Lifecycle") -- Score: 57/100

**Current opening:**
